## Video Editing Tips (For Maximum Engagement)

### Audio Strategy
- **Hook (0-3s):** Use attention-grabbing sound effect or controversial statement
- **Build (3-20s):** Calm, conversational tone with strategic emphasis on key numbers
- **Climax (20-40s):** Faster pacing, more dramatic sound design
- **Close (40-60s):** Slow down, direct address, thought-provoking question

### Visual Strategy
- **Cut every 2-3 seconds** (keeps viewers watching)
- **Use on-screen text for all key stats** (80% watch without sound)
- **Include graph animations** for growth/market share data
- **Show driver/rider testimonials** if possible (social proof)
- **Contrast visuals** (Uber branding vs Empower, expensive ride vs cheap ride)

### Color & Branding
- **Black background** for text-heavy sections (contrast + modern)
- **Uber black/white** vs **Empower brand colors** (visual contrast)
- **Green for savings** ($$$), **Red for opportunities** (alert)
- **Consistent font** (Sans-serif, bold for readability at high speed)

### Trending Sounds
- Morning Brew uses: Energetic, slightly comedic background music
- Avoid: Oversaturated sounds (choose trending but underused sounds for algorithm boost)
- Pair with: Natural pauses in voiceover

## Expected Engagement Metrics

### If Done Right (High-Performing Post)
- **Views:** 50K-500K+
- **Engagement Rate:** 8-15% (comments, saves, shares)
- **Shares:** High (controversial topic + actionable question)
- **Comments:** 200-1000+ (debate format drives discussion)

### Optimization for Algorithm
1. **Hook in first 3 seconds** (if people don't watch first 3s, algorithm kills reach)
2. **Question at end** (comments = engagement, engagement = distribution)
3. **Share-worthy** (make people want to tag friends)
4. **Watch time** (aim for 45-60s scripts to train longer watch times)
5. **Mobile-first** (9:16 aspect ratio, large text, no small graphics)
