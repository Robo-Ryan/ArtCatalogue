## Video Editing Notes (UPDATED)

### Visual Storytelling Strategy
- **Hook (0-3s):** Empower winning (positive, surprising)
- **Build (3-20s):** Introduce Waymo, show driverless footage
- **Twist (20-40s):** The realization - driver advantage disappears
- **Close (40-60s):** Timeline + question (creates urgency)

### Key Visuals to Include
1. **Empower app** (modern, clean interface)
2. **NYC market share graph** (upward trending)
3. **Waymo autonomous vehicle** (sleek, futuristic)
4. **Price comparison graphic** ($20 human vs $8 driverless)
5. **Timeline animation** (2026 → 2027 → 2028 → 2029)
6. **Driver → Robot transition** (visual metaphor for the threat)

### Audio/Pacing Changes
- **First 10s:** Upbeat (celebrating Empower success)
- **10-25s:** Shift tone (introduce the threat)
- **25-40s:** Building tension (the math, the timeline)
- **40-60s:** Direct, analytical (strategic clarity)

### Sound Design
- Start with energetic sound (Empower winning)
- Shift to ominous tone (Waymo introduction)
- End with thoughtful, deliberate pacing (the real question)
